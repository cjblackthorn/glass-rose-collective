# About Glass Rose Collective

Glass Rose Collective is an indie-focused creative collective built to support artists trying to create meaningful work outside of exploitative systems.

We are not interested in turning artists into disposable content.

We care about:

- sustainability
- authenticity
- collaboration
- emotional honesty
- artistic identity
- long-term creative growth

Glass Rose Collective exists as a hybrid of:

- indie publishing house
- creative support network
- artist representation hub
- collaborative collective
- community-driven creative ecosystem

Some artists may work directly with us.
Others may simply need guidance, referrals, support, or trusted connections.

If we cannot help directly, we want to help creators find someone who can.

## Who We Support

We support creators across multiple disciplines, including:

- writers
- poets
- musicians
- photographers
- visual artists
- designers
- alternative creators
- underground artists
- disabled creators
- LGBTQ+ creators
- emotionally raw and unconventional creative voices

## Why The Name?

Glass Rose Collective represents beauty that survived fracture.

The stained-glass imagery reflects individuality, vulnerability, imperfection, and resilience — separate pieces forming something meaningful together.
