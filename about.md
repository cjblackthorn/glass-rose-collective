  </p>

<div class="grc-editorial-section">

    <h2>Why Glass Rose Exists</h2>

    <p>
      Too many creative spaces have become transactional, disposable, and emotionally hollow.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="grc-editorial-panel">
    <p>
      Glass Rose Collective was created to build something smaller, more intentional, more collaborative, and more human.
    </p>

    <p>
      We believe art still deserves humanity, sustainability, vulnerability, identity, and care.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="grc-dark-section">
  <h2>How We Work</h2>

  <p>
    Glass Rose Collective is intentionally selective and project-based.
  </p>

  <p>
    Some creators may collaborate directly with us. Others may simply need guidance, publishing support, networking opportunities, referrals, or trusted creative connections.
  </p>

  <p>
    We are not interested in exploitative systems, mass-produced creative pipelines, or disposable creator culture.
  </p>
</div>
